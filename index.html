Before you step inside

A few ways to explore

Take your time, look around, and enjoy the memories.

⌨

Move around

Use W A S D or the arrow keys. Hold Shift + W to run forward faster; hold Shift with another direction to run that way.

↔

Look around

Drag with your mouse, or swipe the scene on a touch screen.

◎

Discover the gallery

Stand close in front of a frame, face it, then press E or click it to see its photo and quote. Find the upstairs album to browse every photo.

⌑

Sit and explore

Use F near the bench. Find the screen to choose a video.

↑

Visit the upper floor

Go behind the central wall on its right side, then walk up the stairs.

♧

On a phone or tablet

Move with the on-screen joystick and use the action button.

♡

Make it yours

Use the top buttons for reactions, camera views, and settings.
